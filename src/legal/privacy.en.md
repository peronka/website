## Introduction

Peronka (the “App”) is an independent mobile app for iOS and Android. It provides timetables and real-time train departure information for passengers travelling on the Polish rail network. Timetables come from the public data published by PKP Polskie Linie Kolejowe S.A. through PKP PLK OpenData.

This policy covers the App and the website peronka.pl (the “Website”). It explains what information we collect, how we use it and what rights you have. Please read it before using the App.

## Data we collect

The App is designed to minimise data collection. You do not need an account or have to provide your name. You give us your email address only if you apply to test the Android app, sign up for a launch notification or write to us. We process the following types of information.

In the App:

- Connection and search data. Peronka’s server receives your device’s IP address and the query, such as the station name you entered or the number of the station you selected. We use these to find stations and retrieve timetables.
- Installation ID and preferences. On first launch, the App generates a random installation ID. Peronka’s server uses it to manage settings, favourite stations and tracked trains. Some settings and widget data are stored on your device. Your device generates notifications about delays and platform changes.
- Analytics. The App sends app opens, screen views and events to Peronka’s server, such as adding a favourite station or starting to track a train. Events include the time, platform, app version and installation ID. Usage events are also sent to Google Analytics for Firebase. Firebase may process information about your device and operating system, and infer your approximate location from the connection. Analytics collection begins when the App starts. The advertising consent form does not turn off these analytics.
- Location on Android. If you allow approximate location access and use the nearest-station feature, the App sends coordinates to Peronka’s server to find the station and calculate the distance. You can also select a station manually. You can turn off location access in Android settings.
- Advertising data. This includes choices made in the Google UMP consent form and, depending on the platform, your consent and the advertising configuration, connection data, interaction data, information about how the SDK works and device advertising identifiers. Section 5 gives more details.

On the Website:

- Visit statistics. Vercel Web Analytics collects the page URL, referring URL, country, device type, operating system and browser. It distinguishes visitors using a hash derived from request data that resets daily. This prevents it from tracking the same person across days or websites.
- Google Analytics statistics. If you consent in the cookie banner, we collect pages visited, traffic source, visit duration, events (such as clicking the App Store badge), device type, operating system, browser and approximate location. These are associated with an identifier in the `_ga` cookies. Google Analytics does not store IP addresses.
- Server logs. The hosting provider automatically records the IP address, date, time and URL of each page requested. The status page fetches data directly from Peronka’s server, which also receives the browser’s IP address.
- Closed Android testing. The form collects the email address for the Google Play account you submit.
- Launch notifications. The “Notify me” form collects your email address and the dates and times of signup and confirmation.

In correspondence:

- Email. We receive the sender’s email address, the contents of the message and any information provided voluntarily, such as a device model included in a bug report.

- Handling complaints, under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR (a legal obligation under the Polish Consumer Rights Act).
- Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims, under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (the Controller’s legitimate interest).

## Advertising and consent

The App is free and uses Google AdMob and the Google UMP consent platform. Android test release 0.5.1 has no ad unit configured to display production ads.

Where required, the App displays the Google UMP consent form. You can agree to personalised ads and the associated data processing, select specific purposes or decline consent. On iOS, access to the advertising identifier (IDFA) requires permission through App Tracking Transparency. On Android, you can reset or delete the advertising identifier in system settings.

Without consent to personalisation, ads are non-personalised and selected using general context, such as an approximate location inferred from your IP address.

You can change or withdraw advertising consent at any time in the App’s settings. You can change tracking permission in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before you withdrew consent.

Google acts as a separate data controller for personalised ads. See [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Google uses profiling to select personalised ads. The Controller does not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you (Article 22 GDPR).

## Cookies

The App does not use cookies. Google Mobile Ads and Firebase libraries store information needed for their operation on your device, such as the installation ID and consent choices. The advertising identifier is read only as described in section 5.

The Website stores cookies only with your consent in the cookie banner. The Google Analytics cookies `_ga` and `_ga_<identifier>` distinguish visitors and their sessions and expire after two years. Without consent, the Google Analytics script does not load. We remember your choice in the browser’s local storage so we do not ask again on every visit.

You can change or withdraw consent at any time through “Cookie settings” in the Website footer. Withdrawing consent removes the Google Analytics cookies. Vercel Web Analytics operates without cookies or identifiers stored in the browser.

## Data retention

- Connection data and server logs are kept for as long as necessary for security and service stability, for up to 12 months.
- Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days after collection.
- Google Analytics data for the App and Website is kept for up to 14 months after collection. The `_ga` cookies expire after two years or earlier if you withdraw consent.
- For Website visit statistics, the hash used to distinguish visitors is deleted after 24 hours. Afterwards, only aggregate statistics that cannot identify an individual are retained.
- Advertising consent choices stay on your device until you change them or remove the App. Google processes data for personalised ads in accordance with its own policies.
- Android test applications are kept until testing ends or you withdraw consent, whichever comes first. You can withdraw by emailing [kontakt@peronka.pl](mailto:kontakt@peronka.pl).
- Launch notification subscriptions are kept until the launch email is sent or you withdraw consent, whichever comes first. An address is added to the mailing list only after confirmation through the email link.
- Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle the matter. For complaints, it is kept until the limitation period for related claims expires.
- You can remove preferences stored on the device by uninstalling the App. To request deletion of data stored by Peronka’s server, contact the Controller at [kontakt@peronka.pl](mailto:kontakt@peronka.pl).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can contact the Controller at any time to exercise the following rights:

- Access your data and obtain a copy (Article 15 GDPR).
- Rectify inaccurate data or complete incomplete data (Article 16 GDPR).
- Request erasure, also known as the “right to be forgotten” (Article 17 GDPR).
- Request restriction of processing (Article 18 GDPR).
- Have your data transferred to another controller (Article 20 GDPR).
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21 GDPR).
- Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
- Lodge a complaint with the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, Poland).

Connection data is necessary to retrieve timetables. You can select a station manually without granting location access. Declining Google Analytics in the Website’s cookie banner does not limit Website features. You can withdraw consent to launch notifications at any time by emailing [kontakt@peronka.pl](mailto:kontakt@peronka.pl) or using the unsubscribe link in the email.

## Security

The Controller applies technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risks and the categories of data processed. Communication between the App and its servers uses TLS 1.2 or newer. Only the Controller has access to server infrastructure, and all administrative operations require multi-factor authentication.

Despite these precautions, no IT system is completely immune to security incidents. If a personal data breach poses a high risk to users’ rights or freedoms, the Controller will promptly notify the affected individuals and the competent supervisory authority.
